Sole Miles — private gear mileage, without accounts.

Track mileage for running shoes, walking shoes, boots and other distance-worn gear. Log activities against the gear you used, see per-item odometers and a last-four-weeks rotation view, set your own retire targets, and archive old gear without losing history. Back up your records to JSON, restore a validated backup with an explicit confirmation, or export CSV when you choose.

Sole Miles stores your ledger offline on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, and Windows. No account, ads, social feed, location tracking, or cloud sync. Retire targets are personal maintenance reminders, not medical or injury advice.

Windows portable downloads will be available on tagged GitHub releases after release validation. Mobile store releases are planned; App Store Connect registration remains blocked pending developer agreement acceptance.
